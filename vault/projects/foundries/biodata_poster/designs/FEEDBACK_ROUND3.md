# John's feedback on round 2 (pitch posters)

Applies to every round-3 poster.

## Keepers

John is close to happy presenting these three Typst run-receipt posters:

- `typed-not-guessed-receipt`
- `known-unknowns-receipt`
- `skills-are-binaries-receipt`

What he said about each:

- **skills-are-binaries:** probably his favorite poster, and the metaphor is good. But:
  - the Z-Report takes up too much space;
  - the "oracle" note is a little too technical.
- **known-unknowns (draft format):** very strong. "Rails, not reminders" is his favorite way any poster has used the Orbit paper. But the full Donald Rumsfeld quote is too much.
- **typed-not-guessed:** a lot of good in it, but it has the weakest side points and spends too much space on lists in the middle. Also:
  - swap the order of "JOBS RUN 0" and "wall time" so the row doesn't read as if nothing works;
  - don't show the whole Nextflow workflow in the typing section.

## Fixes for all posters

1. **Orbit 0/15.** Leading with "0/15" makes a bad first impression; it reads as failure.
   - Don't make 0/15 a hero number.
   - Frame the finding positively: the rails/tools decide, and prompts alone don't.
   - Supporting sources, from `research/r7-biorxiv-preprint.md`, all real:
     - "Skills are guidance, not enforcement" (preprint p.16);
     - the static connection validator caught an agent's bad `tximport` wiring before execution (p.4, pp.15–16, Fig S3A);
     - Orbit now refuses by default (post-v0.8.0).
   - 0/15 may stay as small, attributed context, or be dropped.
2. **QR codes.**
   - No QR code for the lightning talk(s): neither `gcc2026-lightning` nor `foundry-pattern-in-one-minute`.
   - The Foundry and preprint QR codes are required on every poster.
   - Others are optional, e.g. the full slides or gxformat2 docs.
3. **Authors.** Add Scott Cain (Penn State, affiliation ¹) to every poster, before Anton Nekrutenko, who stays last:
   > John Chilton¹, Marius van den Beek¹, Dannon Baker², Danielle Callan³, Scott Cain¹, Anton Nekrutenko¹
4. **Priority.** Case studies (DengueSeq etc.) and the draft-format detail are low priority. Use them only as padding when space allows. (The exception is a poster whose own receipt *is* the draft format.)
5. **The pitch.** Build better skills, build better workflows, and keep the receipts. A literal title along those lines is allowed.
6. **Density.** Keep it low; the round-1 complaint still stands. Body text is ≤ 400 words total.
