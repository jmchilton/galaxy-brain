## General

- He likes the **tape** metaphor for the receipts.
- All of the top 5 need **higher information density**, except perhaps `skills-are-binaries-receipt`. The round-3 cap of 400 body words is lifted. Size floors still apply: text ≥ 24 pt, body text ≥ 28 pt, hero ≥ 100 pt.
- A poster should also tell a reader what the Foundry *does* and how to *use* it, not only show evidence.
- Explore a more traditional poster direction:
  - keep the color scheme, the receipt edges and the tape;
  - lay out the content in boxes of conventional information: what it does, how it works, the story from the Orbit paper, and a pipeline diagram for Nextflow → Galaxy and paper → Galaxy conversion;
  - mix the receipts in with those boxes.
