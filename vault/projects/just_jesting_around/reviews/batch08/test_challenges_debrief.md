## Isolation and abstractions

The visibility helper observes real registrations, removes only captured `visibilitychange` callback/options pairs, and restores its spy after the store stops. It preserves the existing history repair and follows it into the concrete notification consumer.

The new chat factory provides the five required ChatHistoryItem fields and accepts typed overrides; chatStore and ChatModeSelector replace sparse casts while keeping IDs visible in their scenarios. Existing notification fixtures and tooltip timing helpers already fit their consumers. No additional shared abstraction is needed, and no best-practice addition is justified by these examples.
