## One server, three clients

- **Galaxy:** the stage 3 worktree's (port 8091, its own SQLite database, user demo@example.org). Its backend is stage 2's plus the `preferred_output` step field. The demo tool below was added to its toolbox for this capture only.
- **Clients:** each stage's own Vite dev server, proxying to that Galaxy: stage 1 on 5183, stage 2 on 5182, stage 3 on 5181. Stage 1 has no Embed Outputs button, and stage 2's client ignores `preferred_output`.
- **One invocation per workflow:** each workflow was run once, with its stage 3 preferred outputs set, and viewed through each client. A stage 2 frame and a stage 3 frame of the same view therefore share the invocation, history and chrome; they differ only in what each stage draws.

## Demo content (P1)

The demo tool, `demo_content` ("Demo content: for layout replicas: copies prepared demo files, not tool results"), takes any number of upstream datasets (ignored, so the edges stay), and copies prepared files to up to three outputs and a list. Each output's format (txt, tabular, bed, html, png, bam, bigwig) and, for tabular, column names come from parameters. It lives in the session scratchpad with its content generator; neither is committed.

The content is synthetic, generated by a script with a fixed seed. It is shaped like each real output, and every plot carries the words "synthetic demo data".

## Checks

- **Flips line up.** Stage 2 and stage 3 frames of one view share the anchor to within 0.15px; the moves read off the drivers' node positions match the documented ones: on ChIP-seq at 50%, filter MAPQ30, Call Peaks, summary of MACS2 and Bigwig are 24.5 screen px (49 workflow px) right in stage 2; in stage 3 only summary of MACS2 moved, 17.5 screen px (35 workflow px, documented as 34). On ATAC-seq, remove comments lines is 82 screen px (164 workflow px) left in stage 3. In the MACS2 stack, Call Peaks and Bigwig are 39 and 40 px left, summary of MACS2 12 px.
- **Toggling off restores the layout (P8).** After toggling off, every step's position equals its authored one, and the restored frame matches the before frame at zero shift; 2,106 of 1.6 million pixels differ (antialiasing from the refit) on ChIP-seq, 779 on the test workflow.
- **Running frames.** For both stages, the driver compared every step's and card's bounding box before and after `final` finished: unchanged.
- **Card content.** A probe recorded, per card, the rows shown in full and the image box: tables show five rows (one before P2), the demo plot is 238×119 in a 256×137 body, and grown steps show four rows below their label.

- The first stage 2 running close-up cut off the `final_table` card; retaken centred on the card too.
- The running close-ups' graph sits lower in the canvas than before, so the deck crops the band holding the three steps rather than the top of the frame.
