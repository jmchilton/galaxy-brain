The backend `FetchUploadToolAction._setup_job` replaces each `src: "files"` node, in recursive encounter order, with the next uploaded file. Previously the collection builder reversed flat-list elements while leaving its file array unchanged. Moving reversal before payload construction means both lists derive from the same ordered items. The production call sites use `uploadCollectionDatasets`, not the low-level builder directly. Both current collection callers use a shared abort signal, making the batch atomic; the helper also preserves optional per-file signal and progress-ID alignment. It copies list arrays before reversal, leaving caller-owned items, upload IDs, and signals unchanged. Standalone upload submission and its URL/paste handling are outside this change.
