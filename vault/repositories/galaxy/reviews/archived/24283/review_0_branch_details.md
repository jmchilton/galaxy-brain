`pr_24283_review_0` builds on the PR head `27544cf8f0c`. It tightens the lifetime of pooled Whoosh searchers. When a newer commit is seen, every stale idle searcher is closed, not just the popped one. `ToolWhooshIndex` gains a `close()`, which `CachedToolboxSearch` calls on panel searches it drops or replaces; searchers returned to a closed index after that are closed rather than pooled. The freshness check now reuses Whoosh's own TOC and segment filename patterns. The branch also adds the missing tests: a tuning change replaces the panel search, and a searcher that raised is never re-pooled. Tests are red-to-green, or were mutation-checked where they pass on the PR head.
